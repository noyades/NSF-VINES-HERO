# Kickoff Meeting Agenda

**Date:** October 23, 2026  
**Core meeting:** 30 minutes  
**Optional spillover:** up to 15 minutes

## Objectives

- Align on near-term hiring milestones and owners.
- Identify the next platform enablement and site synchronization decisions.
- Compare available tapeout paths at Chalmers and Virginia Tech (VT).
- Agree on a recurring monthly meeting cadence.
- Leave with clear actions, owners, and due dates.

## Core Agenda: 30 Minutes

| Time | Topic | Discussion focus / desired outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:02 | Welcome and goals | Confirm attendees, desired outcomes, and timebox. |
| 0:02-0:07 | Hiring timelines | Review roles, hiring sequence, target dates, dependencies, and recruitment owners. Identify any decisions or blockers. |
| 0:07-0:15 | Platform enablement and site synchronization | Review platform readiness, site prerequisites, synchronization approach, and outstanding dependencies. Agree on the next technical validation and who will coordinate it. |
| 0:15-0:23 | Tapeout plans: Chalmers / VT options | Inventory available processes, facilities, design support, lead times, costs, and decision criteria at each site. Identify information still needed before choosing a path. |
| 0:23-0:27 | Recurring monthly meetings | Choose a cadence, preferred time window/time zone, meeting owner, and agenda/minutes process. |
| 0:27-0:30 | Miscellaneous and close | Surface urgent additional items; recap decisions, action owners, and due dates. Defer non-urgent items to follow-up. |

## Optional Spillover: 30-45 Minutes

Use only for topics that need more working time; keep the 30-minute core focused on decisions and next steps.

| Time | Topic | Possible focus |
|---|---|---|
| 0:30-0:38 | Technical deep dive | Resolve open platform or site synchronization questions and define a validation plan. |
| 0:38-0:43 | Tapeout comparison | Compare Chalmers and VT options against agreed criteria; assign follow-up questions to local contacts. |
| 0:43-0:45 | Action review | Confirm owners, due dates, and topics for the next monthly meeting. |
